# Campaign brief: Quenchline contain mode for platform teams

**From:** Noor Eskelund, Product Marketing
**Date:** November 2, 2026
**Status:** Approved to plan
**Reviewers:** Priit Salonen (claims), Kofi Anselm (developer content, X)

## What is happening

Most Quenchline customers still run in observe mode. We want platform teams who have been watching for a few months to switch on contain mode. This is a product-adoption push, not a launch.

## Goals

Internal, not for copy: 30 customers moving at least one cluster to contain mode by June 30, 2027, and 15 demo requests from prospects.

## Audience

Primary: platform leads at existing Quenchline customers who run observe mode today.
Secondary: platform leads at prospects who have read about Quenchline but worry it will break production.

## Key messages

1. Observe mode tells you what Quenchline would have done. Contain mode does it.
2. The containment actions are specific: isolate a pod's network, freeze a process, drain a node.
3. It's lightweight and does real-time containment, so nothing slows down.
4. Proof: Lowmoor Credit Union went from 4 hours to 11 minutes. That's about 20x faster, use the multiple, it lands harder. Put Ostler's 63% next to it so it reads as one big result.
