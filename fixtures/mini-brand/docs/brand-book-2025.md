# Ferrowell brand book

Issued June 2025 by the Ferrowell marketing team.

Ferrowell makes route-planning software for regional cold-chain delivery fleets: grocery, pharmacy and food-service distributors running 20 to 400 refrigerated vans.

## Name and tagline

- The company is **Ferrowell**. One word, capital F. Never "FerroWell" or "Ferrowell Inc." in marketing copy.
- The product is **Ferrowell Routes**. On second mention, "Routes".
- Tagline: **"Cold stays cold."** Sentence case, with the full stop. Use at most once per piece.

## Voice

- Plain and specific. Drivers and dispatchers read our posts; write so a dispatcher at 5am understands it.
- Lead with what changes on the road: fewer late drops, fewer temperature excursions, shorter shifts.
- No exclamation marks. No "revolutionary", "game-changing", "AI-powered".
- Numbers need a source in the same sentence.
