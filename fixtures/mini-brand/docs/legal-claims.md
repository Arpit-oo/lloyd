# Claims we can and cannot make

Owner: Legal, reviewed August 2025.

- Ferrowell holds **no FDA certification**, and there is no such certification for routing software. Never say "FDA certified", "FDA approved" or "FDA compliant". We may say: "Ferrowell Routes keeps a temperature log for every delivery, which helps customers with their own FDA food-safety records."
- Only customers on the product overview's cleared list may be named. Harlow Foods is not cleared.
- No prices in public copy until the pricing page launches.
- Do not claim fuel savings; we have no audited figure.
