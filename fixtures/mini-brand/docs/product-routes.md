# Ferrowell Routes: product overview

Updated September 2025.

Ferrowell Routes plans delivery routes for refrigerated fleets and re-plans them during the day when a stop runs late or a van's cargo temperature drifts.

## What it does

- **Temperature-aware routing.** Routes reads each van's reefer sensor every 60 seconds and moves temperature-sensitive stops earlier when a unit starts to drift.
- **Live re-planning.** When a stop runs more than 15 minutes late, Routes proposes a new order for the rest of the day. A dispatcher approves it; Routes never changes a driver's route on its own.
- **Proof of temperature.** Every delivery gets a temperature log the customer can download, from loading to hand-over.

## What it does not do

- It does not control the refrigeration unit. It reads sensors; it never sets temperatures.
- It does not do warehouse or inventory management.
- It supports vans with Carrier, Thermo King or Zanotti units with a telematics gateway. Other units need a third-party gateway.
